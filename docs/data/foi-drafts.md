# FOI request drafts

These are drafts for the maintainer (@terddyy). **Nothing here has been sent.** Submit them through the eFOI portal (https://www.foi.gov.ph/) or the agency's own FOI channel, and record the reference number and date in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).

Common header for each request (the eFOI form asks for the same fields):

- Requester: BetterMarikina maintainers (civic transparency project, https://github.com/BetterMarikina/bettermarikina)
- Purpose: To publish the information, with attribution, on a non-commercial public transparency website for Marikina residents.
- Preferred format: Electronic copy (PDF, XLSX or CSV), searchable text where possible.

---

## FOI-1: Barangay officials of Marikina City (DILG-NCR / DILG Marikina Field Office)

**Why:** The city contact page lists only the Punong Barangay for each barangay and is dated 2023. Kagawad and SK Chairperson names, and any vacancies or successions since the October 2023 BSKE, were not found in an official source. (Sprint 01, Phase 3.)

**Request:** A list, as of the date of reply, of the incumbent Punong Barangay, seven Sangguniang Barangay members, Barangay Secretary and Treasurer if available, and SK Chairperson for each of the 16 barangays of Marikina City, noting any vacancy, suspension or succession since 30 October 2023. Please include each barangay hall's official office telephone number or email.

**Also:** the status of the terms of office after RA 12326.

## FOI-2: Certified 2025 election results for Marikina City (COMELEC Marikina Election Office)

**Why:** The councilor list on the city's contact page matches secondary sources, but the city's own "Our City" page still shows the previous council. We want to confirm against the certified results. (Phase 4.)

**Request:** The Certificate of Canvass or proclamation list for the 15 May 2025 elections in Marikina City: city mayor, vice mayor, the two House representatives and all 16 Sangguniang Panlungsod members, with proclamation dates.

## FOI-3: Searchable copies of Full Disclosure Portal documents (Marikina City Budget Office / City Treasurer / CPDO)

**Why:** Most PDFs on https://fdpp.marikina.gov.ph/full-disclosure are scans without a text layer, so figures cannot be extracted reliably. (Phase 6.)

**Request:** Spreadsheet (XLSX/CSV) or text-searchable copies of, for 2020-2026: the Annual Budget (summary of income and appropriations by sector or office), Statement of Receipts and Expenditures, Statement of Indebtedness, SEF utilization, 20% component of NTA utilization, LDRRMF utilization, GAD Accomplishment Report and Annual Procurement Plan and Bid Results.

## FOI-4: City resolutions and ordinance dates (Office of the City Council Secretary)

**Why:** The portal lists 149 ordinances (series 2000-2026, with gaps) and one resolution, with no approval dates. (Phase 5.)

**Request:** An index of all ordinances and resolutions of the Sangguniang Panlungsod from 2019 to date, with number, title, date approved and author or sponsor, plus the names of the standing committees and their chairpersons for 2025-2028.

## FOI-5: Department heads (HRMO / Personnel Office)

**Why:** Department heads in the 2023 Facts and Figures are out of date. (Phase 4.)

**Request:** The list of current department and office heads of the City Government of Marikina (name, position, office, and official office telephone/email).

## FOI-6: DPWH projects in Marikina City (DPWH Metro Manila 1st District Engineering Office)

**Why:** The DPWH website did not return content to the tools used in Sprint 01. (Phase 10.)

**Request:** The list of DPWH projects located in Marikina City for 2020 to date: project name and location (barangay), approved budget for the contract, contractor, start and completion dates, and status.

## FOI-7: Flood hazard levels and evacuation centers by barangay (Marikina CDRRMO)

**Why:** No official per-barangay list was found. (Phase 11.)

**Request:** The list of designated evacuation centers per barangay and the official water-level thresholds (alarm levels) of the Marikina River with the date they were last revised.

**Sprint 04 note:** PAGASA's flood forecasting site gives the station thresholds (Sto. Niño 15 / 16 / 17 m), so the request can focus on the city's own alarm table (news reports put the 3rd alarm at 18 m) and each evacuation center's name, address and the barangays it serves. Someone already filed a similar eFOI request ("List of evacuation centers in Marikina City and Quezon province"); check its answer when the portal is back before sending ours.

## FOI-8: Roadmaps for Districts I and II (City Planning and Development Office)

**Why:** The PDFs at https://www.marikina.gov.ph/facts/District%201.pdf and District%202.pdf are image-only and could not be read. (Phase 1.)

**Request:** Text-searchable copies of the District One and District Two Roadmaps.

## FOI-9: Garbage collection schedule by barangay (City Environmental Management Office)

**Why:** No official collection schedule is published online; the Ecological Profile only says pickups are thrice weekly. (Sprint 04, topic 5.)

**Request:** The current solid waste collection schedule for each of the 16 barangays (collection days and time window, by area or street if the barangay is split, and which days are for biodegradable, residual and recyclable waste), plus where residents can bring bulky or special waste.
